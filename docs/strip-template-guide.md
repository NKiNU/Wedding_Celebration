# Photo strip template guide

For whoever designs the template. Upload it in **Admin › Photo strip template**.

## File
- **1080 × 1920 px** exactly (9 : 16, the Instagram story size).
- PNG or JPG, up to **3 MB** (export as JPG if a PNG is too big).
- Full screen: the background and the strip in one image.

## Photo windows
- Draw 3 empty boxes where the photos go (any colour).
- After uploading, drag the 3 windows onto your boxes in Admin and set their size.
- The photos are placed **on top** of your design, so nothing drawn inside a window shows.
- Default windows: 500 × 375 px each, x 290, y 250 / 665 / 1080.

## Keep clear for Instagram
Instagram covers the **top 250 px** (name bar) and the **bottom 340 px** (reply bar).
Keep the photos and the couple's names between them.

## What guests save
Exactly this image at 1080 × 1920 with their 3 photos in the windows. No app buttons on it.

The built-in default template also gets the couple's names and the wedding date drawn on it by the app.
A custom template is used as it is, so put the names and date in your design.
